We are proud to share our results from the **Washington State Rabbit Breeders Association (WSRBA) Annual Convention**! Our Netherland Dwarfs brought home multiple **Best of Variety** ribbons.

**Show A results:**

- Group 1 — REW, Best of Variety
- Group 2 — Sable Point, Best of Variety
- Group 5 — Orange, Best of Variety
- Group 5 — Fawn, Best of Variety

These wins reflect the dedication behind our selective breeding program, prioritizing both show-quality type and wonderful temperament. Thank you to the WSRBA for hosting a wonderful convention, and to the judges for their thoughtful evaluations. Congratulations to all who competed!
