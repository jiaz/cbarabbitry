## Waitlist Policy – Effective March 1, 2026

To ensure responsible breeding practices and proper planning of our litters, CBA Rabbitry operates on a waitlist-based breeding system, breedings will be planned based on demand from our waitlist.

Our goal is to produce healthy, high-quality Netherland Dwarf rabbits while avoiding overbreeding and ensuring every bunny has a committed home.

### How Waitlist Works
When you join the waitlist, you'll tell us your preferences, such as gender, coat color, quality (pet or show), and your preferred adoption timeframe. As babies become available, we will contact families on the waitlist in order.

Being on the waitlist You have the previlege to **pre-select your bunny before it is listed to the public**.

If none of the available bunnies are the right fit, you're never required to choose. You can simply pass and remain on the waitlist for the next litter until the perfect bunny comes along. Our goal is to help every family find the bunny that best fits their expectations, rather than rushing them into a decision.

This flexible process allows you to wait for the right bunny while keeping your priority position on our waitlist.

### Waitlist Deposit

The waitlist deposit is $49. After you adopt a bunny from us, the waitlist fee will be applied toward the total price.

### How to Join

Please use the payment button below to submit your waitlist fee and preferences.

### Waiting Time

Our typical wait time is approximately 2–6 months, depending on the current size of our waitlist and your preferred bunny characteristics. In general, the more specific or rare your preferences are, the longer the wait may be.
We encourage families to join the waitlist as early as possible, When your turn comes you can either choose a bunny or pass and remain on the waitlist for future litters.

### Our Commitment

At CBA Rabbitry, we focus on producing:

- Healthy rabbits
- Strong Netherland Dwarf bloodlines
- Exceptional temperament
- Breeding to the SOP

We appreciate your patience and support in maintaining a responsible and ethical breeding program.
