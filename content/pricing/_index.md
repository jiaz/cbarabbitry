### How Do We Determine Our Prices?

Our pricing is based primarily on two factors: **coat color and conformation**. Rarer coat colors and more desirable physical features generally command higher prices.

- **Coat Color:** Rare and sought-after colors, such as Fawn, Creamy, Sable Point, and Light Lilac, generally start at $599.
- **Conformation:** Bunnies with highly desirable Netherland Dwarf features, such as a large, round head and exceptionally tiny ears, generally start at $599.

The final price reflects a combination of coat color and conformation. At CBA Rabbitry, every bunny is special. Our pricing reflects each bunny's individual qualities and appearance, helping you find the right little companion for your family.

The waitlist deposit is $49 and is applied toward the total adoption price when you adopt. See our [Join Waiting List](/join-waiting-list/) page for details.
