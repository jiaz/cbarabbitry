## Adoption Pricing

Our bunnies are priced by grade, based on conformation, bloodline, and rarity. Every bunny — regardless of grade — is raised with the same love, daily handling, and care.

| Grade | Quality | Price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Grade A — Adorable** | Adorable companions; may have some conformation variation. | $199–$349 |
| **Grade P — Premium** | Pet quality with great conformation, such as a round face; has a show-line parent. | $399–$549 |
| **Grade S — Signature** | Exceptional conformation with show-line parents, short ears, big round face, or rare coat colors. | $599+ |

The waitlist deposit is $49 and is applied toward the total adoption price when you adopt. See our [Join Waiting List](/join-waiting-list/) page for details.
