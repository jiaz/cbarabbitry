Interested in bringing home one of our bunnies? Fill out the form below, then pay the reservation fee to complete your request.

After payment, we'll get back to you to start the adoption paperwork within 2 hours. Please include any questions or requests for more photos in the form. We want to make sure every adoption is the right match for both the bunny and your family.

Please review our [Sales Policy](/sales-policy/) before submitting your request.
