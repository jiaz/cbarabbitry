Bunny Adoption Pricing

Our Netherland Dwarf bunnies are priced from $449–$799, depending on coat color and conformation. (How Do We Determine Our Prices?) Sometimes we offer special promotional adoption prices lower than $449 on darker-colored bunnies, which are great budget-friendly options.

(** Very occasionally, we have exceptional, top-quality bunnies priced above this range, which we classify as Breeder-Keeper Grade. You need contact us directly if you are interested in this grade.)

All our bunnies:

  • ✅ Are raised with love indoors
  • ✅ Receive daily socialization by CBA staff
  • ✅ Have showline with legs
  • ✅ Every family who adopts a CBA Rabbitry bunny receives lifetime support and our Bunny Donkey-Face Protection. We’re here to support you throughout your bunny’s life.
